The rock began far from Earth. Collisions can break larger asteroids apart, leaving fragments that travel around the Sun on their own orbits.

03

Crossing Earth’s orbit.

  1. 01

    Orbit the Sun

    The rock traveled around the Sun on an elongated orbit that crossed Earth’s orbital path.

  2. 02

    Enter Earth’s atmosphere

    Earth and the rock reached the crossing point at the same time. The rock heated up and broke apart as it descended.

  3. 03

    Examine the surviving rock

    Recovered fragments preserve veins of melted rock formed during earlier impacts.

reach the ground.
The surviving pieces are meteorites. The main mass from Chelyabinsk was recovered from Lake Chebarkul.

Saw it fall or found it later?

People saw the Chelyabinsk fireball. Later, pieces were recovered from the ground and from Lake Chebarkul.
